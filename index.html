How to Play

🎯 Objective

Race all 4 tokens from your base, around the board, and into your home triangle. The first player to bring all four tokens home wins.

🎲 Rolling & Moving

Tap the dice or the Roll button on your turn. A token may leave base only on a 6. Rolling a 6 grants an extra roll. If no token can move, your turn passes automatically.

⚔️ Captures

Landing exactly on an opponent's token sends it back to its base. Tokens on safe spots can never be captured.

🏁 Home Column

After a full lap, a token turns into its coloured home column. It must land exactly on the final square to finish.
